## 2. Part A: stored bite written in advance

**Problem.** When an enemy enters the square beside an idle Bane cell, the enemy bites first 47.4% of the time beside a
holder and 64.8% beside a cell resting under 5 energy (`m-r1-first-contact.txt`, table 2). A cell whose stored bite
already points at the square is bitten first 2.0% (`m5-refill-killer.txt`, no other enemy). About 45 such contacts per
game; first-order estimate about 12 extra first bites per game, up to about twice that (inferred).

**Change (rule corrected 2026-11-13).** On the rest paths that have an empty neighbour (prey hold, settled rest
below 5 energy, blocked walker below 5): when gate 1 sees a refill pattern (its `thr_tbl` entry is 12: an empty
neighbour with an enemy beside or beyond it), store a bite on gate 1's first-choice square (its `act_tbl` entry). With
no refill pattern, write nothing, so a killer keeps its bite on the square it emptied and far cells are untouched. An
enemy that enters the stored square is then bitten by the 4-instruction stored-bite check at `start:`. 74% of enemy
entries beside a holder go into that square (`m-r1` table 2b).

**Implementation constraints.**
1. Take the square from the gate tables by mask, never from `al`: at `rest:`, `al` is 0 on the boxed path
   (`test al, al / jz boxed`) and a feed action on the feed fallback (`ja rest`). Boxed cells then store nothing by
   construction (no refill entry).
2. Recompute the refill mask inside the rest path (the settled path overwrites `ecx` with `VIEW_X`). Do not save it
   earlier: an extra instruction before the gate exit or on the rule-4 paths would lengthen racing paths and move the
   39 walker-move marker.
3. Do not test the stored square for "empty". `CELL_EMPTY` is 0 and so is "no stored bite" (byte [3] = 0 reads view
   byte 24, always 0), and an inherited bite (copied as a relative offset) can point at any empty square. Either would
   block the write for good.
4. The late-game split (`VIEW_TICK >= 2980`) and every path that leaves through `EXIT_ADDR` must be unchanged.
5. Only `cmova` / `cmovae` are on the site's allowed list; no `cmove` and no other new instruction classes.

**Known small loss.** A killer with two or more refill squares whose emptied square is not the first choice has its
bite moved. Bound: 284 of 3,645 entries beside a resting non-holder went to a square other than the first choice
(`m-r1` 2b), about 8%. Accepted; a freed-set table could remove it later.

**Cost.** Every racing path keeps its count (stored bite 4, first bite 12, refill 16, split 16, walker move 39). Rest
paths gain about 8-10 instructions (exact counts from `check_cells`). A rest races nothing.

## 3. Part B: opening

- Default: "first child long 8/2, emptiest side" (`tools/search/opening_search.py`, `make(dir_rule='open')`). Solo from
  the 11 sampled start squares (`m-solo-matrix.txt`): never below v2.2 at any square or tick; t60 +5.2% (equal starts),
  +7.7% (bad), +4.5% (good); window-a boxed 11.8 -> 10.1% (equal).
- First a short solo search around "open-long 16/2, stop after 4 steps with no friend in view" (+21.6% t40, +10.2% t60
  on equal squares, but -30% at t20 on two corner squares, -0.7 to -4.8% at t100 on three). Accept a variant only if it
  is never below v2.2 from t20 to t60 on all 11 squares and at most -1% at t100.
- The real cell must match the sketch within 1% at t30 and t60 on the 11 squares; report the new path counts.

## 7. Honest size

Incremental. It reaches the close equal-start games (two of five t100 gaps are -154 and -188). It does not close the
large gaps, the bad starts (our opening near walls: solo reproduces them) or Siect-F (bites under 4 from the first
tick). Confidence that v2.3 flips at least 1 in 5 equal-start games against VarSi-type rivals: 40% (deep review).
