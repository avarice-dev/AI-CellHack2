## The task: a cell that beats seeding, not a copy of it
Starting points, in the order I would weigh them:
- **Seeding is geometry, and geometry can be out-seeded.** Our feeding cannot buy perimeter; blobs can. The solo
  growth simulator (`sim/growth.py`, trusted before contact) can compare seed counts, distances and bearings in
  minutes; it needs a move-capable policy (the engine supports ACT_MOVE; growth.py policies today only split and
  feed). Target curve: t50 462, t100 1486 (124335). Beating it means more or better-placed blobs, e.g. seeds along
  the longest free axis and toward the far corners, a third generation, or shorter steps earlier.
- **Their scouts are fragile.** A 2-energy cell walking a straight line for 15 ticks dies to one bite and cannot
  act while stunned. We cannot reach them in t1-31 from one blob, but our own seeds could be placed to meet theirs.
- **Keep what already works**: Bane's hold (no 2-3-energy children in contact), the F5 bite, the 11-instruction
  refill, capped-donor feeding after the board fills. Drop what costs income without buying perimeter: Schism's
  outward feed at e >= 6 during the opening (CogBloomXT rests 79-95% of the time in t0-100).
- **Conversion** (finding 4) is the second lever: a border cell with a freed enemy square beside it splits into it
  instead of biting when the split child would be >= 8; refill child size 10+ once the border is fat.
- Memory is needed for scouts: generation, bearing, steps left. Contagion (`cells/gen_contagion.py`, scouts at
  EDGE=10, STEPS=10, S<=2: the conditions that made it do nothing) has the memory machinery and the move path;
