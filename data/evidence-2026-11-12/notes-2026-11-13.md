- notbest: beside a contested square, but another of our cells beside it was the best neighbour.

- hct 543 = best 535 + 8 holders with an enemy on an edge (the budget view counts those as front).

So the 395 are 193 notbest + 186 freed + 24 uncontested. None of them is a threshold-16 hold (the 12+ column is
0-9 per game).
