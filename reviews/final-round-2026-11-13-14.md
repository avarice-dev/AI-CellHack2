The first interpretation ("lowering the near-enemy split gate costs growth") was withdrawn after review. Root cause of the V2/V3 collapse: they were mis-specified. They set `threat_min=5`, which is Gate 1 in `make_scourge` and is not enemy-gated, so every cell with an empty neighbour split unconditionally at e >= 5 in open space and Bane2's opening walker/dispersal was disabled (`moves = 0` in all four finished V2/V3 games against 587-1392 for V0, V1 and split10; the same "no walking" collapse as in `data/opening-search-2026-11-11.md`). So V2/V3 never tested what they meant to test. The corrected variants keep `threat_min=16` and gate the e >= 5 split on a real enemy.

### Why checked refill (V0) was worse than it looked

- The first-bite cost rose from 19 to 22 instructions (26 if the old byte-2 square is occupied, 28 if e < 12). On seed 2 the trace is identical to split10 through t=14 and diverges at the first contact at t=15: split10 gets `deaths=0, eats_enemy=1`, V0 gets `deaths=1, eats_enemy=0`. t60/t100 were 566/609 for V0 against 775/989 for split10.
- Friendly fire: newborn refill children are bitten by already armed siblings. At t=120 armed-friend bites were 205 / 249 / 154 (seeds 1 / 2 / 6) for V0 against 107 / 95 / 82 for split10; over 3000 ticks V0 had `arm_fr` 7188 and 6764 (seeds 1, 2) against 642 for split10 on seed 2 (seed 3 was the exception: split10 5109, V0 887). 61-85% of early armed-friend bites hit refill children.
- Hold off (V1) pushed early deaths up (`deaths_before_t100` 93 / 90 for V0 to 138 / 139 for V1) because cells near enemies with 5 <= e < 16 run the rotating walker instead of a clean split.
- Against Siect-F the original Misfortune already won the bite order at the front 89% to 11% (game 146477 in `data/siectf-games.md`) while losing the equal-area growth race (`data/siectf-growth-2026-11-13.md`). That is the standing diagnosis: contact is won, growth is lost.

### Armed children (AC0/AC1/AC2) and the 1000-tick screen

- Idea: a newborn split beside an enemy is armed from its first tick, so it bites in 2 instructions on tick+1. Parity rule: arming only at e & 3 == 1, so a failed split (parent stays at e) can never leave the child's even energy slots under the parent's own armed range.
- Early (pre-restart, old code) signal on `ac2 s1`: 1871 final cells against 1837 for V0 and 1619 for split10; armed kids won the first bite 74.6% against 16.5% unarmed. Caveat: alive-after-10 for armed kids (65.3% against 81.7%) was confounded by e = 5 giving a child at energy 2.
- Instruction-cost review changed the design: enemy-near test first, then `e & 3 == 1`, then direction scan, then store. Open-space splits pay about +2; arming on proven-near paths costs +21.
- Reference/asm fidelity rule adopted: the reference may only read view bytes its asm path really loads (Gate 1 at e >= 16 did not load bytes 2/22; fixed with a unit test).
- Bug found by the screen: `parent_fired_child_slot` (pfc) = 1 on seed 6 (t=71 to 72, cell (45,24), e=4). A boxed neighbour's Schism feed (94-96 insns) ran before the e=5 dispersal split (97 insns) and bumped 5 to 7, so the split produced child 3 (odd, unarmed) and parent 4, which is an armed child slot at n=2: the parent bit its own newborn. Fix: `MISFORTUNE_FEED_PAD = 6` so that the minimum Schism feed is at least 100 insns, above the longest armed split (97 to 98). The pad is only safe while every armed split path stays below 100 insns.
- Reference bug found in review: the feed pad was applied to the control `make_misfortune2` too (missing `armed_children and`); fixed and the gate restarted on 6 workers.

## 4. Depravity (cell 363): build, live failure, hotfix analysis

Build c4d7350: locked spec = 2-insn armed bite, 15-insn Gate 1, `arm_tbl` (arm 19 to 17 via `jmp [arm_tbl + rax*8]`), `pat1_tbl` k=0 stun-save arming only {e0-4, e0-2, e0}, Cap B deleted, `HOLD_MIN = 8`, first 8/2 dir-open (pure SSE2 plus a 256-byte `pop8_tbl`, no popcnt, ecx preserved), `T_FEED = 200` (boxed cells feed only at cap before tick 200).

Verification: `check_cells --only depravity` 38,960 cases / 0 mismatches, binary 18,602 B, planted mutation (K_SHORT = 3) caught with 3,779 mismatches. Counts met: armed 2, prefix+scan 9, arm 17, gate 1 15, hold_split 29 (32 at tick >= 2980), walker 38, boxed below T_FEED 23/33. Over spec (accepted as built): settled dir-open 76-139 insns (mean about 91) against about 35-85 expected, and standard boxed at tick >= 200 38-86. Site Sandbox 151538 (500 ticks, vs Misfortune v3, Bane v4, Conviction): Depravity 1st, pop 138 (Bane 43, Conviction 42, Misfortune v3 33), 0 faults, insns/call avg 57, max 135 (Misfortune v3: avg 84, max 144). One game, not a rating. It was laddered beside 353 at the user's go (and recreated once with a different icon).

Live failure: in its first game against AtomVirus and VarSi it failed to push either back. Root cause as analysed by the reviewer (not verified on the ladder):

1. Without Cap B there is a deterministic 48 <-> 50 stalemate on fed front cells. At e = 48 the 2-insn armed bite gives +2 to e = 50; with byte 63 = 0 (slot 50 unarmed) and no Cap B, the cell at e = 50 falls into `arm:` (17 insns), VarSi (4 insns) or AtomVirus (4-5 insns) stun it back to 48, and it repeats with no forward push. Misfortune v3 avoids it because Cap B drains rear friends from 48 to 46.
2. `T_FEED = 200` starved engaged fronts (e = 16..46) during t50-t200 while AtomVirus and VarSi fed their fronts continuously.

Hotfix d1637f3 (local only): Cap B restored exactly as in misfortune2, `T_FEED` / `BOXED_HEAD` removed, arm_tbl 17, k=0 stun-save, HOLD_MIN 8 and dir-open kept. Binary 23,610 B, 38,960 cases / 0 mismatches, planted Cap B pad mutation (31 to 30 xor) caught with 11 problems. Counts: armed 2, arm 17, gate 1 15, cap B pull 80, boxed 77-140. The measuring run produced a finding against the reviewer's root cause: the front cell with an adjacent enemy is unchanged by the patch (e = 48 gives 2 insns, e = 50 gives 17 insns, action 0x31, before and after), because an adjacent enemy sends it to the bite/arm path before the cap rule. The patch only changes the boxed rear cell (e = 48/50 goes from 38 insns, rest, to 80 insns, cap B pull), i.e. it helps indirectly by draining the rear donor. The local checks cannot show whether that ends the stall. Decision (user): the hotfix does not address the front-cell loop, so it was not uploaded; 363 stays with the original build. Lesson recorded for Dogma: a fix for the 48 <-> 50 loop must act on the front cell itself (a bounded re-arm, see K_CAP below).

The local deficit is the 3-insn against 2-insn bite trade-off itself, not an implementation bug. Swapping the +2-lane `rotate_pick` for dir-open or clockwise picking leaves the same 3 of 11 solo regressions.

### Verdict

Dogma meets its build spec exactly and fixes what it was designed to fix (0% feed before t100, no faults, beats Bane v2.2 everywhere, beats every rival proxy in duels). It does not beat the live #2, Misfortune v3, on our own engine, and none of the three authorised knobs changes that. NO-GO: no upload, cells 353 and 363 untouched, commits stay local. Any remaining improvement would lie outside the three knobs (the 3-insn against 2-insn combat bite).

## 6. Decisions and rules worth keeping

- Ship only after the slot-balanced, paired comparison against the live control (split10), never against a control that already carries the regression under test.
- A live sandbox loss against the previous version is a stop signal.
- Reference and asm must read the same view bytes at the same instruction counts; a unit test must fail if the reference reads a byte its asm path does not load.
- Any change that adds instructions to the 2 / 15 / 17-insn fast paths needs a measured reason; AC1 and Dogma both lost to cells that kept the 2-insn bite.
- Bounded re-arming (K_CAP) is the front-cell answer to the 48 <-> 50 stall; K_CAP 25 had half the lock time of 16 at no placement cost.
